Driving the Cloud Security Flywheel
Cloud Security Portfolio · Seller Walkthrough
Cloud security isn't a point solution.
It's a progression.
This is the platform story — not four products, one motion. A single successful deployment expands naturally into the next security outcome. Walk the journey; each phase reveals the problem that comes next and the part of the portfolio that answers it.
~5 minutes · 4 phases · platform story
The Maturity Curve
Every step, you control a little less.
Same journey, one lens: how much you actually own shrinks at each phase. The platform is what keeps visibility and protection constant as it does.
Network
FortiGate VM
You own it — traffic between cloud and on-prem, and the cloud edge, segmented and inspected end to end.
Control plane
FortiCNAPP
You don't own the infrastructure — it's self-service from a portal — but you still influence how every service is configured and restricted.
Apps & APIs
FortiWeb + FortiAppSec
You don't control who shows up — users, bots, AI agents — only how the app and its APIs respond.
SaaS
FortiCASB SSPM
You control almost nothing but the data you put there and which identities can reach it.
← You own itYou only govern the data →
Span of control shrinks at every step — that's the maturity curve. You want all of it eventually; you can't do it at once. Phase the approach: land where control is highest and risk is most contained, then expand along the curve. The platform is the constant that carries visibility and enforcement with you.
The Flywheel Motion
Every deployment reveals the next challenge.
The goal is all of it. The path is one phase at a time — land the first, fund and de-risk the next.
Each phase funds and de-risks the one after it: more visibility → more protection → more operational context → more platform adoption. The seller's job is to land the right first phase and name the one that comes next.
